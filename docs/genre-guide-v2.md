# Genre labeling guide v2.0 (DRAFT, 2026-10-28; Mahdi must freeze it before any v2 labeling or run)

**Status: DRAFT.** This guide is for GenreHalluEval v2 only. It splits the five joint genres of guide v1.0 into 11
single genres (Mahdi's decision, 2026-10-28), plus a 12th genre, GRAMMAR (Bangla grammar), added by Mahdi the same day. Guide v1.0 stays frozen for BenHalluEval and GenreHalluEval v1.1.

Once Mahdi freezes it, it must not change before any v2 judge result is seen (golden rule 2). If a gap is found
before that, write v2.1 with the date and the reason, and relabel the affected questions.

**What to look at:** the question and the passage only, never the answer. Decide from the question first. Use the
passage only to understand what or who the question is about.

**One genre per question.** v2 keeps only questions that clearly fit one genre. If two genres fit about equally, mark
the confidence `low`. Low-confidence questions are left out of v2 or rewritten.

## Genres (12 + Other)

| Code | Genre | What belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| HISTORY | History | Past events and eras: dynasties, empires, ancient civilizations, rulers before modern states, wars (including the 1971 Liberation War), colonial history, partition, uprisings and mass movements (including 1952, 1990 and July 2024), revolutions, massacres and genocides, the slave trade. People mainly known for such events (for example freedom fighters). |
| POLITICS | Politics and government | The state today and in the past: governments, constitutions, laws and courts, elections, political parties, offices and officeholders (presidents, prime ministers, ministers, MPs, speakers), the civil service, the police and the military as institutions, diplomacy and international bodies (the UN). National symbols. Politicians. |
| EDUCATION | Education | Schools, colleges, universities and madrasas (founding, heads, departments, rankings), education boards, public exams and admission tests, curricula and degrees, libraries. People mainly known as educators. |
| ARTS | Arts and entertainment | Literature (books, poems, plays, authors), film and TV, music of every kind (including folk music and bands), dance, painting, sculpture, theatre, comedy, photography, beauty pageants. Awards for these (Oscar, Grammy, Bangla Academy award). People mainly known for these. |
| CULTURE | Culture and language | Festivals and fairs that are not religious observances, customs and rituals of everyday life, food and drink, clothing, crafts and folk traditions (other than music), the Bangla calendar. Language outside Bangla grammar: scripts (including the history of the Bangla script), other languages and their alphabets, meanings of words that belong to no other genre, proverbs. Philosophy. |
| SPORTS | Sports | Games, teams, clubs, leagues, tournaments, the Olympics, records, players, coaches, referees, sports bodies (ICC, FIFA) and sports venues. |
| SCIENCE | Science and health | Physics, chemistry, biology, the human body, medicine, health and diseases, drugs, mathematics, astronomy (planets, stars, black holes), earth science (rocks, minerals), animals, plants, natural phenomena, energy and the environment as concepts. Scientists. |
| TECHNOLOGY | Technology | Computers, the internet, software, programming languages, AI, electronics, phones and telecom, TV and radio as devices, satellites, rockets and space agencies, instruments (microscopes, telescopes), vehicles, power plants, engineering, inventions and inventors, technology companies and their founders. |
| GEOGRAPHY | Geography | The natural world on the map: continents, oceans, seas, rivers, lakes, mountains, passes, deserts, forests, reefs, islands, deltas, landforms, climate of a region. Countries and regions as places (location, borders, area, population, nicknames, capitals, official languages, currencies). Administrative units (divisions, districts, upazilas) and cities as units. Natural tourist spots. |
| PLACES | Places and landmarks | Named sites that people built or that are known from archaeology: buildings, monuments, mausoleums, bridges, roads, railways and metros, canals, ports, airports, stadium buildings only when asked as buildings, parks, museums as buildings, archaeological sites and ruins. Their location, size, construction, designers, opening and old names. |
| RELIGION | Religion | Religions and beliefs, scriptures, verses and religious terms, worship and rituals, religious festivals and observances, gods, prophets, gurus, saints, monks and religious scholars, the events and people of early Islamic history (the Prophet's life, the early caliphs, Badr, Hudaybiyyah, Khandaq, Karbala), Islamic law and its schools, Sufi orders, epics, Puranas and mythologies, religious groups and reform movements. Temples, mosques and shrines when the question asks about their religious role. |
| GRAMMAR | Bangla grammar | Bangla grammar and usage: sandhi and sandhi-bichchhed (সন্ধিবিচ্ছেদ), samas, karak and bibhakti, prakriti and pratyay, upasarga, parts of speech (pada), dhatu, bachya, kal, linga, bachan, purush, ukti, ণত্ব-ষত্ব বিধান, correct spelling, punctuation, Bangla sounds and letters (ধ্বনি, বর্ণ, যুক্তবর্ণ), sound changes, sadhu and cholit bhasha, synonyms and antonyms, এককথায় প্রকাশ, বাগধারা, and the type or source language of a Bangla word (tatsama, tadbhava, deshi, bideshi; for example, "আনারস" and "চাবি" came into Bangla from Portuguese). |
| OTHER | Other | Only when nothing above fits: economy, banks and business (not technology), media, crime, accidents and disasters, refugees, everyday trivia. v2 does not use Other questions. |

## Rules for unsure cases (apply in this order)

1. **A question about a person** (birth, death, family, real name, education, career, events in their life) takes
   the genre the person is mainly known for. A footballer's birthplace is SPORTS; a writer's PhD is ARTS; a
   politician's birth name is POLITICS.
2. **History or Politics.**
   - A dated event (a war, uprising, massacre, coup or referendum) is HISTORY.
   - Offices, officeholders, parties, elections, laws, courts and government bodies are POLITICS, whatever their
     date. For example, "who is the current prime minister" and "who was the last prime minister of undivided
     Bengal" are both POLITICS.
3. **Science or Technology.**
   - Knowledge about nature (what something is, how nature works, health and medicine) is SCIENCE.
   - Things people build, and the people and companies that build them, are TECHNOLOGY.
   - For example, the electron and insulin are SCIENCE; the transistor, the laser and NASA are TECHNOLOGY.
4. **Geography or Places.**
   - Natural features, countries, regions and administrative units are GEOGRAPHY.
   - Built or excavated sites are PLACES.
   - A river is GEOGRAPHY; a bridge over it is PLACES.
5. **Religious sites.**
   - A temple, mosque, shrine or monastery is RELIGION when the question asks about its religious role, rites or
     religious history.
   - It is PLACES when the question asks about its location, size, construction or ruins.
6. **Arts or Culture.** A specific work, performer or artistic genre (including folk music) is ARTS. A tradition,
   custom, festival, food or the language itself is CULTURE.
7. **Festivals.** A religious observance (Eid, Durga Puja, Rath Yatra, Christmas, Buddha Purnima) is RELIGION. A
   non-religious festival (Pohela Boishakh, a book fair) is CULTURE.
8. **Words.**
   - The meaning of a word takes the genre of the word's field. For example, "robot" is TECHNOLOGY and "dengue" is
     SCIENCE.
   - The grammar of a Bangla word is GRAMMAR, whatever the word means: its type (tatsama, tadbhava, deshi, bideshi),
     the language it came from, its sandhi or samas, its prakriti and pratyay, its part of speech. For example,
     "Which language did আনারস come from?" is GRAMMAR, while "What fruit is আনারস?" is SCIENCE.
   - Grammar terms are GRAMMAR. Words with no other field are CULTURE.
9. **Organizations** take the genre of their field. A cricket council is SPORTS, a ministry is POLITICS, a
   university is EDUCATION, a technology company is TECHNOLOGY, and a bank is OTHER.
10. **Still unsure:** mark `low`. In v2, `low` questions are left out or rewritten so that they fit one genre.

## Confidence

- `high`: one genre clearly fits.
- `low`: two genres fit about equally, or the topic is unclear.
