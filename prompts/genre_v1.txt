ARTS = Arts, Culture & Sports: literature, film and TV, music and dance, painting and sculpture, theatre, folk culture, festivals and food; language features, grammar and scripts; philosophy; sports (games, teams, clubs, tournaments, the Olympics, players, coaches, sports bodies); people mainly known for any of these.
SCIENCE = Science & Technology: physics, chemistry, biology, the human body, medicine and health, mathematics, computing and the internet, inventions and engineering, agriculture, animals, plants and natural phenomena; technology companies; scientists and inventors.
GEOGRAPHY = Geography & Places: countries, regions, cities, districts, capitals and headquarters; rivers, mountains, glaciers and seas; area, population and administrative units; landmarks, buildings and infrastructure (location, size, when built, opened or renamed, who designed them, old names); the languages a country speaks; a country's currency.

OTHER = only when nothing above fits: economy, banks and non-technology business, banknotes, media, accidents and disasters, everyday life.

Rules for unsure cases, applied in this order:
1. A question about a person (birth, death, parents, spouse, education, career) takes the genre of the field the person is mainly known for. A writer's birth date is ARTS. If the person is known for several fields, use the one the question or the passage names first.
2. A named place and its properties is GEOGRAPHY. Exceptions: a religious building is RELIGION; a sports venue is ARTS; an event that happened at a place is HISTORY; schools and universities follow rule 5.
3. A general natural concept (layers of the atmosphere, cells, genes) is SCIENCE. A specific named river or glacier is GEOGRAPHY.
4. "Who was first", "what was first" and "who invented" take the genre of the thing: the first computer is SCIENCE, the first rock band is ARTS, the first independent king is HISTORY.
5. An organization takes the genre of its field: a cricket council is ARTS, a spy agency is HISTORY, a technology company is SCIENCE, other companies and banks are OTHER. Schools, colleges, universities and libraries are HISTORY, unless the question asks about one as a place (area, location), which is GEOGRAPHY. A museum takes the genre of its collection.
6. The meaning or origin of a word takes the genre of the word's field. Grammar terms, and words that belong to no other field, are ARTS. A language's own features, script or history are ARTS. Which languages a country or people speaks is GEOGRAPHY.
7. National symbols (flag, anthem, emblem) are HISTORY. Gods, characters and events of the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the Puranas and other mythologies are RELIGION; literary works that retell them are ARTS.
8. If you are still unsure, pick the best fit.
