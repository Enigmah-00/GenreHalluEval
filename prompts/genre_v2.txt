Rules for unsure cases, applied in this order:
1. A question about a person (birth, death, family, real name, education, career, events in their life) takes the genre the person is mainly known for. A footballer's birthplace is SPORTS; a politician's birth name is POLITICS.
2. A dated event (a war, uprising, massacre, coup or referendum) is HISTORY. Offices, officeholders, parties, elections, laws, courts and government bodies are POLITICS, whatever their date.
3. Knowledge about nature, health and medicine is SCIENCE. Things people build, and the people and companies that build them, are TECHNOLOGY.
4. Natural features, countries, regions and administrative units are GEOGRAPHY. Built or excavated sites are PLACES: a river is GEOGRAPHY, a bridge over it is PLACES.
5. A temple, mosque, shrine or monastery is RELIGION when the question asks about its religious role, rites or religious history, and PLACES when it asks about its location, size, construction or ruins.
6. A specific work, performer or artistic genre (including folk music) is ARTS. A tradition, custom, festival, food or the language itself is CULTURE.
7. A religious observance (Eid, Durga Puja, Rath Yatra, Christmas, Buddha Purnima) is RELIGION. A non-religious festival (Pohela Boishakh, a book fair) is CULTURE.
8. The meaning of a word takes the genre of the word's field. The grammar of a Bangla word (its type, the language it came from, its sandhi or samas, its part of speech) is GRAMMAR, whatever the word means. Grammar terms are GRAMMAR; words with no other field are CULTURE.
9. An organization takes the genre of its field: a cricket council is SPORTS, a ministry is POLITICS, a university is EDUCATION, a technology company is TECHNOLOGY, a bank is OTHER.
10. If you are still unsure, pick the best fit.
